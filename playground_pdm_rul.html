Introduction to Remaining Useful Life on the course's own seven pumps. Tab one: where the label comes from — a pump that ran to failure gives every hour a label, the hours left until that failure; the model works inside a 720-hour planning horizon; a pump still running has no label yet. Tab two: the health indicator — pumps sit at different healthy vibration levels in mm per second, so the reading is smoothed over 24 hours and z-scored against the pump's own baseline window. Tab three: one linear regression from the indicator to the hours left, fitted on the four failed training pumps and read out step by step at any testing hour of a new pump. A scope control on the predict tab compares dropping the hours beyond the horizon against capping their label at 720: capping lays over a third of the training hours on a flat line and makes the late predictions worse.
