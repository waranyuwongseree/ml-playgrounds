One pump that runs in more than one operating mode, and what that does to a baseline. Every tab reads the same four rows: the machine's hours clustered into one, two, three and four modes. One mode is Day 3's plain mean baseline. The first tab shows how the hours split. The second builds the expected temperature from each split and reads the residual and the control limit it leaves. The third lets the clock run into a fault and scores each split in the operator's units: hours to the first alarm, hours of warning over the fixed seventy five degree limit, and the share of the faulty hours it flags.
