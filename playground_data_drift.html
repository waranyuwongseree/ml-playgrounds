Model monitoring in the three levels of the Day-10 workflow, on one drifting deployment. One vibration feature, two classes trained once and frozen; five levers drift the deployed data. Level 1 watches the input alone: the feature is binned and the Population Stability Index adds one term per bin, with the workflow's 0.1 retrain trigger. Level 2 watches the model's own output before any label arrives: the alarm rate against its three-sigma control band. Level 3 waits for the labels and reads recall against its own band. The same drift looks different at each level, and the blind spots between them are the lesson.
