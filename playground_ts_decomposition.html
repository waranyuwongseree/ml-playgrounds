Hourly electrical load over 14 days. Build sets the level, the trend slope, the daily seasonal shape (sine, square, sawtooth or workday), its amplitude, the noise and how the season behaves, and reports the share of variation each part owns. Window m averages m hours and reports how much of the daily season survives and how far the averaged trend is from the true one. Recover runs Classical decomposition or STL, additive or multiplicative, and scores the recovered seasonal effect against the one that was built. Baselines forecasts 24 or 48 hours ahead from a fixed origin with Naive and Seasonal Naive and reports MAE in kW with a verdict.
