Backtesting with a rolling forecasting origin. The series is built from a trend slope, a season size, a wandering level and a noise level. One origin cuts the series at one point, forecasts forward and reports the error there, next to the smallest and largest error obtainable by moving the cut. Rolling repeats the cut at every possible position and reports the error separately for each horizon, so the growth of error with horizon can be measured. Compare runs all four methods over every cut and reports each one as a percentage of the naive forecast, with a t statistic that says whether the difference is larger than the run to run noise.
