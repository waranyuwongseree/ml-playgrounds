One pump that runs at several load levels, and one pump watched through five sensors at once. The first tab compares three ways of building an expected vibration on five recorded files: a single baseline for the whole machine, a regression on flow, and a separate baseline for each load mode found by k-Means. The second tab asks whether the machine really has modes at all, by comparing the sample standard deviation against the moving-range estimate and by reading the silhouette of k equals two, three and four, with a switch for whether the rows were shuffled before clustering. The third tab watches five sensors together and compares the largest single z score, Hotelling T squared and the reconstruction error Q, every limit set at the same percentile of its own healthy distribution, with a score plot of the first two principal components coloured by truth and a switch for whether the model learned from the healthy rows only or from the whole file.
