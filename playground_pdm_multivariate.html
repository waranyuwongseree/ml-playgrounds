Two sensors on one machine that normally rise and fall together, and what happens when a fault moves one of them without the other. The first tab is the data itself: both sensors hour by hour beside the same hours plotted against each other, with the square each sensor is judged inside on its own and the ellipse the pair is judged inside. The second follows the chosen rule through every hour against its limit. The third scores both rules in the operator's units: hours to the first alarm, the share of faulty hours flagged, and how many weeks in forty carry a false alarm.
