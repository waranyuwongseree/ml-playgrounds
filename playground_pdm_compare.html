Four hundred motors read at a speed and a torque, and four models fitted on the same rows and scored on the same hold-out: k nearest neighbours, logistic regression, a decision tree and naive Bayes. The first tab is the motors themselves with no model on them. The second draws the region the chosen model calls acceptable and rings the units it gets wrong. Three machines are on the rail, and each of them is answered best by a different model.
