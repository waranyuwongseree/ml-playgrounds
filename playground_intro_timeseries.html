Introduction to time series in four steps. First build a series yourself from its components — a level, a trend, a weekly seasonal pattern, a known external variable x and noise — and read the sum in the table. Then pick a lag and see the correlation between today and that many days ago as a scatter of pairs. Then read the same correlations for every lag at once as the ACF, and with the shorter lags removed as the PACF. Finally choose the model orders p, d, q, the seasonal P, D, Q at period seven and whether the known external variable x enters, fit the model, and read the fit and its check side by side: the forecast on the left, and on the right the residuals, their correlogram and the cross-correlation with x -- what is left says which order (or x) is still missing. A dataset control offers three classic processes, an AR(1), an MA(1) and a random walk, besides the series built from the levers.
