Introduction to time series in four steps. First build a series yourself from its components — a level, a trend, a weekly seasonal pattern, a known external variable x and noise — or pick one of six fixed example series, and read the sum in the table. Then pick a lag and see the correlation between today and that many days ago as a scatter of pairs. Then read the same correlations for every lag at once as the ACF, and with the shorter lags removed as the PACF, each with its Bartlett significance band. Finally choose the model orders p, d, q, the seasonal P, D, Q at period seven and whether the known x enters, fit on the days before the holdout, and read the fit and its check in one column: the forecast against the held-out truth with a seasonal-naive baseline and a scoreboard, then the residuals, their correlogram and its verdict.
